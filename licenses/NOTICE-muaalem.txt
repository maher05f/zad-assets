Changes made for Zad (a Derivative Work under the QML):
- Exported from PyTorch to ONNX (opset 17, dynamic time axis), float32.
- The SeamlessM4T/w2v-BERT feature extraction (Kaldi-style 80-bin log-mel fbank,
  per-mel normalization, stride-2 stacking to 160) was re-implemented in PyTorch and
  embedded in the graph, so the model takes raw 16 kHz audio.
- Kernel-size-1 (pointwise) Conv1d layers rewritten as the equivalent matrix multiplication.
- muaalem-fp32.onnx: full float32, no quantization; weights in muaalem-fp32.onnx.data
  (published in parts because of the 2 GB file limit; concatenate the parts in order).
- muaalem-int8.onnx: weight-only 8-bit quantization of the MatMul weights (onnxruntime
  MatMulNBits, symmetric, block size 128); computation stays in float32.
- muaalem-levels.json lists the output levels and their vocabularies, from the model's tokenizer.
